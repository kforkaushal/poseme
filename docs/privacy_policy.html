Privacy Policy

App: Pose Me! - Camera Pose Guide • Package: com.poseme.app • Effective Date: September 19, 2026

🛡️ Privacy at a Glance

Pose Me! is built with a strict offline-first, privacy-by-design architecture. We do not collect, track, store, or sell any of your personal data, images, or camera feeds. All photo captures, pose outlines, sketch generation, and aspect ratio cropping occur 100% locally on your device.

1 Information We Collect (Or Don't Collect)

We believe your private photos should remain strictly private. When using Pose Me!:

  • No User Accounts: You are not required to create an account, register, or provide personal details (name, phone number, email address).
  • No Image Uploads: Photos captured through the app, live viewfinder previews, and imported gallery images never leave your device. We do not operate cloud servers for image storage.
  • No Behavioral Tracking: We do not track user browsing habits, screen interactions, or pose usage patterns.
  • No Advertising Trackers: The application does not integrate third-party ad networks or data broker SDKs.

2 Device Permissions & How They Are Used

To provide camera pose guidance and saving functionality, Pose Me! requests specific Android permissions strictly for app functionality:

Permission Technical Purpose Data Shared?
CAMERA Renders the live camera viewfinder and captures photos with interactive pose guide overlays. Never (processed strictly on-device)
INTERNET Fetches curated, public pose reference photos and inspirations from the Pexels API. None (only fetches public image URLs)
WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Saves captured and cropped photos directly to your device's picture gallery (Android 9 and below). Never (stored only on local device storage)

3 On-Device Processing & Machine Intelligence

All image processing features operate completely offline inside background worker threads (Dart Isolates):

  • Artistic Sketch Generation: Edge contour detection and silhouette extraction are executed entirely by local image processing algorithms on your phone's processor.
  • Aspect Ratio Cropping & Watermarking: High-resolution bicubic resizing, square/portrait/cinematic cropping, and brand watermark compositing occur purely in local memory.
  • Local Disk Caching: Downloaded reference poses and computed sketches are cached locally in your application's private storage directory for instant offline access and can be cleared at any time.

4 Third-Party Services

Pose Me! interacts with a single third-party service solely to deliver pose inspiration content:

  • Pexels API: Curated royalty-free reference poses are retrieved via public HTTPS queries to the Pexels API. No user personal information is sent to Pexels. Use of reference images complies with Pexels' content license terms.

5 Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR Compliance)

Pose Me! does not target or knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because the app collects zero personal data from any user, it is fully compliant with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

6 Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not collect or store user data on remote servers, there is no remote data retention. Any photos captured or saved in the app reside in your device's local gallery or application cache. You have 100% control over your photos and can delete them at any time using your device's default Gallery app or by uninstalling the application.

7 Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may periodically update our Privacy Policy to reflect future feature updates or legal regulations. Any modifications will be updated on this page with a revised "Effective Date". We recommend checking this page occasionally to stay informed about our privacy practices.
