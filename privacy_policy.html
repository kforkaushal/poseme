Privacy Policy

01 / ARCHITECTURE

01 On-Device & Privacy-by-Design

Pose Me! is designed as a focused, offline-first camera companion tool. We operate under a strict privacy-by-design policy: no accounts, no tracking, and no external photo transmission.

🔒 100% On-Device Processing Guarantee

All camera viewfinder feeds, photo captures, edge-detected contour sketches, and aspect-ratio cropping operations are executed purely inside local worker isolates on your phone's processor. No user photos are ever uploaded to any cloud server or third party.

02 / DATA DISCLOSURE

02 Information We Do Not Collect

To provide absolute transparency regarding your data, we declare the following:

  • No Personal Identifiable Information (PII): We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, IP addresses, or home locations.
  • No Account Registration: Pose Me! is fully functional without logins, passwords, or authentication profiles.
  • No Advertising SDKs or Trackers: We do not include third-party advertising networks, analytics tracking beacons, or cross-app data brokers.
  • No Biometric Recognition: Pose guidance uses pre-rendered silhouette templates and standard 2D edge filters. We do not extract, store, or transmit facial recognition or biometric profiles.
03 / SYSTEM PERMISSIONS

03 Android Permissions & Exact Use Cases

Pose Me! requests only the minimal set of hardware and platform permissions necessary to provide camera guidance:

Permission Technical Purpose Data Shared?
android.permission.CAMERA Powers the live camera viewfinder and captures high-resolution photos with overlay pose guides. Never (processed exclusively in-memory)
android.permission.INTERNET Retrieves curated royalty-free pose reference images and categories via the Pexels API. None (fetches public image URLs only)
android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE Saves captured photos directly to the user's local device gallery (for devices on Android 9 and below). Never (stored strictly on local device storage)
04 / EXTERNAL SERVICES

04 Third-Party Content Providers

The application connects to a single external content provider to deliver pose inspiration catalogs:

  • Pexels API: Curated photography references are fetched via encrypted HTTPS requests to the Pexels API. No user identity or device identifiers are sent to Pexels. Use of reference poses complies with the Pexels Image License.
05 / COMPLIANCE & RETENTION

05 Data Retention & Children's Privacy

Children's Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K): Because Pose Me! collects zero personal data, it does not knowingly collect or solicit information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). The application is safe for general audiences of all ages.

Data Retention & Deletion: You retain complete ownership and control over all photos created. Because photos and computed pose sketches reside solely on your device's local file storage, deleting photos in your device's Gallery app or clearing the app cache completely wipes that data.
